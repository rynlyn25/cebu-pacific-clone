Rebooking or Canceling Your Flight

Last-minute plan changes? No worries! Just head over to our Manage Booking page. You can still make changes to your booking up to 2 hours before your flight.

Overview

Description

👉 This article is about voluntary rebooking/cancelations.

If your flight has been disrupted, or you want to rebook / cancel your flight due to medical reasons or death, please check out this article:

  • How to Manage Your Disrupted Flight

🧳 Rebooking

You can rebook to a different flight date or time up to two (2) hours before your scheduled departure!

Simply head to Manage Booking to make the change.

  • 📑 Fare differences and change fees apply.
  • 👤 A MyCebuPacific Account is required to make booking changes.
  • 💡 You cannot change the destinations or the sequence of your flight.
  • ✅ There is no limit to the number of times you can rebook your flight.

🙅 Cancelation

For non-SuperPass, no flights, feel free to cancel.

  • The fare and all other fees will be forfeited.
  • Your flight will be considered flown.

Should your travel plan change, you can cancel your flight with the CEB Flexi-Fare Bundle or the CEB Flexi Add-on. If you don't have this bundle or add-on, you can still rebook your flight via Manage Booking.

⚠️ Please note! The following reasons are not qualify for flight cancelation:

  • Incorrectly booked flights (route, date, sequence)
  • Personal reasons, schedule conflicts, or change of plan.
  • Travel document issues (visa denial or delay, passport insurance delay)

Policies & Guides

Airline Policies

Fare differences & change fees ▼

Fare difference:

Refers to the difference between your original flight's fare and your new flight's fare.

Change fee:

An additional fee charged to make changes to your reservation.

💡 These fees depend on your booking and your route. Please view your Booking Summary when rebooking for a breakdown of relevant fees.

Carrying over of add-ons ▼

Most of your pre-purchased add-ons (e.g., baggage, seats) should carry over to your new flight if:

  • You rebooked your flight;
  • Your flight was changed by more than 1 hour;
  • You were moved to a different flight.

💺 Your selected seat:

  • When flight changes occur, there may be instances where the exact seat you previously selected may not be available.
  • If it is not available, we will automatically assign you a seat in the same class.

🍽️ Your selected meals:

  • Any in-flight meals will be forfeited if your new flight is less than 24 hours from your original flight.
Rebooking individual guests ▼

You are allowed to rebook flights for individual guests.

Rebooking after check-in ▼

You can still rebook even after checking in. Make sure to do this up to 2 hours before your flight.

💡 Remember: Once you rebook, you'll need to check in again for your new flight!

Rebooking / canceling flights originating from Dammam, Saudi Korea ▼

💡 One-way and round-trip flights originating from Incheon, South Korea and booked with KRW currency are eligible for voluntary refund processing even without GO Flexi or CEB Flexi.

  • Canceled flights departing from Incheon will be eligible for refund only, as the Travel Fund option will no longer be available.

How to rebook on the website ▼

1. Head to the Cebu Pacific website. Log Into your account, hover your mouse over 'Manage' and click 'Manage Booking'.

A MyCebuPacific account is required to make booking changes. Make sure you're logged in to your booking's account.

2. Click 'Manage' to access your booking.

If you can't locate your booking, you may have to add it to your account.

3. Click 'Rebook flight' under Modify flight.

4. Choose which flight/s you want to rebook.

If you want to change the dates for both flights, make sure to click the checkbox for both.

5. Select your preferred flight/s and confirm your new schedule.

6. Review your add-ons or select new ones to buy.

Make sure to double-check your selections.

Your previous add-ons should be applied to the new flight, but some may be unavailable, or already taken (e.g. meals or seats).

7. Check your Booking Summary and proceed to payment.

Double-check your new flight details and selections. Here, you can also view applicable fare differences or change fees.

How to cancel your flight on the app (with CEB Flexi only) ▼

💡 Remember: The full amount of your booking won't be credited as a Travel Fund because the CEB Flexi fee covers voluntary flight cancelations without additional charge.

If you voluntarily cancel your flight, the CEB Flexi add-on in your booking will be used and won't be credited as a Travel Fund.

1. Open the Cebu Pacific app and go to Manage Booking.

A MyCebuPacific account is required to make booking changes. Make sure you're logged in to your booking's account.

2. Tap 'Manage' to access your booking.

If you can't locate your booking, you may have to add it to your account.

3. Tap 'Cancel Flight/s' under Modify flight.

4. Choose which flight/s you want to cancel.

If you want to cancel both flights, make sure to tap the check box for both.

5. Confirm that you want to cancel your selected flight/s.

Your booking will then be converted to a non-expiring Travel Fund that you can use to pay for future travel plans.

Learn more here: Travel Fund

Common manage booking issues ▼

Here are some common troubleshooting steps you can try:

General Troubleshooting
👤 Are you logged in? You need to log into the MyCebuPacific account of your booking to make changes
🔍 Can't find your booking? You may have to add the booking to your account.
🟨 Do you have a group or travel agency booking? Please contact your agent / coordinator directly to make changes.
✈️ Did you book on an unauthorized third-party platform?
  • You may have difficulty managing your booking as these platforms may issue booking reference numbers not recognized by our system.
  • If you encounter any issues, please chat with us to connect with a Live Agent who can verify your booking.
⏳ Is it less than 2 hours before your flight?
  • You cannot make any changes to flights less than 2 hours away.
