Manage disrupted flights anywhere — no need to visit the airport!

👉 This article is about flight disruptions. If you want to voluntarily cancel or rebook a flight

General Guidelines

While we continuously work to ensure your flight departs and arrives on time, on-the-spot delays or cancelations may occur due to environmental, operational or technical reasons.

  • Affected passengers will be notified by Cebu Pacific frontline team, email, and/or text message.
  • You can check the real-time status of your flight through our Flight Status page.
  • We will ensure your welfare is taken care of whenever there are planned or on-the-spot changes to flights.
    • Know your rights as an air passenger through the article "Passenger Rights & Airline Policies"
    • Check the applicable options for on-the-spot disruptions:

After being notified of the disruption, you will be given a link to our Self-Service Disruption Portal (SSDP).

You may also access this by going to Manage Booking on the Cebu Pacific app or website.

📦 Disruption options

We offer the following flexible options for disrupted guests:

  • Rebook your flight
    • Rebook your flight for free up to 30 days before or 30 days after your original schedule.
  • Request a refund
    • Request a refund of your fare according to your form of payment.
    • Travel Tax and unused TravelSure will have to be refunded separately.
  • Convert to Travel Fund
    • When converting your disrupted flight to Travel Fund, it will be stored in your booking reference number or your MyCebuPacific account within 24 hours.
    • With this, you can use it to pay for future flights or add-ons.
    • Once you reach the payment page, simply enter the six-digit booking reference number that contains the Travel Fund.
    • Learn more about the Travel Fund here: CEB Flexi & Travel Fund
  • 💡 Remember!
    • Once you pick an option, it's final and can't be changed or undone.
    • You can only rebook, refund, or convert your disrupted flight to Travel Fund once.
    • When rebooking, make sure you're selecting the correct flight dates!

Policies & Guides

Policies

Deadline for choosing a disruption option ▼
There is no deadline for selecting a flexible option for disrupted flights. However, it is advisable to manage your booking online before the original flight date to avoid having to queue up for a live agent.
Managing booking with multiple guests ▼
To rebook for separate guests, select different disruption options, or split the Travel Fund among yourselves, please chat with us via our app or website to connect with a Live Agent who can help you split the booking.
Changing flight destination ▼

Rerouting or changing flight destination is not allowed.

Rebooking only includes changing the flight schedule.

CEB Super Pass ▼

In the event of flight disruptions, you will be provided options based on the existing policy of Cebu Pacific.

With this, your CEB Super Pass will be voided / will not be returned to you if you decide to rebook, refund, or convert to Travel Fund.

Fare difference ▼

Fare difference is the amount difference between your original booking and the newly rebooked flight. Here's a sample computation for illustration purposes:

Current Booking
(A) Total Ticket Cost Paid PHP 5,500
Rebooking
(B) Ticket Cost PHP 6,300
Fare difference to be paid
(B - A)
(PHP 6,300 - PHP 5,500) = PHP 800
Refund inclusions ▼

Refund includes fares, taxes, all unused ancillaries, Admin Fee, and handling fees (i.e. Unaccompanied Minor), but excludes Flight Fees (i.e. Hold Fee, Cancel Fee, Change Fee).

Guests must coordinate with CHUBB for refund for insurance add-on. CHUBB is our provider for insurance.

Flights booked via Go Rewards will be refunded through Travel Fund.

For more information, visit this article: Refund Concerns

Certificate of Flight Disruption (COFD) ▼

This certificate serves as proof that a certain flight was canceled or affected by a schedule change.

✅ You can request if:

  • Your flight was canceled, or
  • Your departure or arrival time was significantly changed by Cebu Pacific.

❌ You cannot request if:

  • Your flight was completed as scheduled, or
  • Any changes were made at your request (e.g., voluntary rebooking)

🙋 How to request:

Please chat to request a COFD. You will be asked to provide your booking reference number and your last name.

This certificate is free of charge.
