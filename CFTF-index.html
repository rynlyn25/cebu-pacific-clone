Description

🗓️ CEB Flexi

Change of plans? No problem. With CEB Flexi, you can cancel your trip for free.

It allows you to cancel your booking and convert its value to a non-expiring Travel Fund.

  • Cancelation is available up to two (2) hours before departure
  • You can then use this Travel Fund to pay for your future travel plans!

Once you convert to Travel Fund, it will appear in your MyCebuPacific account within 24 hours.

General Guidelines:

  • CEB Flexi only allows for free cancelation of your flight.
  • The full amount of your booking won't be converted to Travel Fund.
  • ✅ Conversion to Travel Fund includes:
    • Fares and taxes;
    • All unused add-ons;
    • Admin and handling fees.
  • ❌ Conversion to Travel Fund does not include:
    • PH Travel Tax, as this is refunded separately;
    • Cost of CEB TravelSure Insurance, as this is refunded separately;
    • Cost of CEB Flexi;
    • Flight fees, such as the hold fee and cancel fee (if applicable).
  • You cannot add CEB Flexi after you've finished booking your flight;
  • The price of CEB Flexi is based on your flight date, and on regulatory guidelines.
  • If you purchased CEB Flexi without a MyCebuPacific account, and you intend to cancel your flight later on:
    • Please sign up or log ii to your MyCebuPacific account first.
    • Remember! An account is required to make booking changes.
    • Check out our how-to guides to know how to voluntarily cancel your flight and convert it to Travel Fund with CEB Flexi.
  • One-way and round-trip flights originating from Incheon and booked with KRW currency are eligible for voluntary refund processing even without GO Flexi or CEB Flexi.
    ⚠️ Important: Beginning May 12, 2026, canceled flights departing from Incheon will be eligible for refund only, as the Travel Fund option will no longer be available.
🟡 Travel Fund

Travel Fund is a non-expiring virtual wallet that you can use to pay for your future trips. It's available if:

  • You purchased CEB Flexi and used it to cancel your flight, or;
  • Your flight was disrupted (i.e. canceled or had a 1hr+ schedule change).
    • How to Manage Your Disrupted Flight
  • Guest/s in the booking has a serious illness or has died;
    • Guest's immediate family member has a serious illness or has died.

General Guidelines:

  • A MyCebuPacific Account is required to make booking changes;
  • Once you convert to Travel Fund, it will appear in your MyCebuPacific account within 24 hours;
  • The currency of your Travel Fund will depend on what currency you used when booking your flight.
  • The Travel Fund can be split among guests in the same booking.
    • With this, please chat with us via our app or website to connect with a Live Agent who can help you.
  • ❌ The Travel Fund cannot be exchanged or converted to cash.

Policies & Guides

CEB Flexi

How to use CEB Flexi ▼

1. Open the Cebu Pacific app and go to Manage Booking.

A MyCebuPacific account is required to make booking changes. Make sure you're logged in to your booking's account.

2. Tap 'Manage' to access your booking.

If you can't locate your booking, you may have to add it to your account.

3. Click on 'Cancel Flight/s' under Modify flight.

4. Choose which flight/s you want to cancel.

If you want to cancel both flights, make sure to tap the check box for both.

5. Confirm that you want to cancel your selected flight/s.

Your booking will then be converted to a non-expiring Travel Fund that you can use to pay for future travel plans.

Cancelling / rebooking CEB Flexi ▼
🖐️ Voluntary cancelation

Please be reminded that pre-purchased add-ons are non-cancelable and non-refundable.

For more information, please visit this article: Rebooking or Canceling Your Flight

💡 Carrying over / transferring of CEB Flexi Add-on

CEB Flexi Add-on should carry over to your new flight if:

  • You rebooked your flight;
  • Your flight was changed by more than one (1) hour;
  • Your flight was canceled, and you moved to a different flight.

CEB Flexi Add-on cannot be transferred to other bookings. Learn more about it here: CEB Flexi Terms & Conditions

Travel Fund

How to voluntarily cancel your booking and convert it to Travel Fund ▼

How to use CEB Flexi to voluntarily cancel your booking

1. Open the href="#">Cebu Pacific app and go to Manage Booking.

A MyCebuPacific account is required to make booking changes. Make sure you're logged in to your booking's account.

2. Tap 'Manage' to access your booking.

3. Click on 'Cancel Flight/s' under Modify flight.

4. Choose which flight/s you want to cancel.

If you want to cancel both flights, make sure to tap the check box for both.

Insufficient Travel Fund ▼

💳 If your Travel Fund is not enough for your flight or add-ons, don't worry!

You can still pay for the remaining amount with a different payment method.

How to check your Travel Fund balance ▼

1. Open the Cebu Pacific app and log in to your account.

2. Go to 'My Account' to view your Travel Fund Balance.

How to check your Travel Fund history ▼

To view your Travel Fund's transaction history:

On the website

1. Log into your MyCebuPacific account.

2. Hover your mouse over "My Account" in the top right corner, then click "Travel Fund"

3. Scroll down to view recent transactions.

On the app

  • Viewing of Travel Fund transaction details is currently unavailable on our mobile app.
How to pay using Travel Fund ▼
✈️ General Guidelines
  • Make sure you're logged into your MyCebuPacific account to use your Travel Fund.
  • If you don't have a MyCebuPacific account, make sure you take note of the booking reference number that contains the Travel Fund.
  • You can use the fund to pay for flights and add-ons.
  • It can also be used to book for your friends and family!
    • To do this, please log into your MyCebuPacific account first.
    • Make sure the person creating the booking is the one who owns the Travel Fund.

Once you reach the payment page, make sure to select Travel Fund.

Travel Fund stored in your booking reference number ▼

If your flight was disrupted and you chose to convert your booking to Travel Fund, it's likely the Travel Fund is currently stored in your booking reference number.

With this, you can still use it to pay for future flights or add-ons.

Once you reach the payment page, simply enter the six-digit booking reference number that contains the Travel Fund.

💡 Remember! If your Travel Fund is insufficient, you can use another payment method to cover the remaining balance.

❗ If you're experiencing an error:

  • Please note that booking reference numbers created by travel agencies may not be used when trying to redeem the Travel Fund.
  • With this, please reach out to the travel agency where your booking was originally created.

Troubleshooting

Common Travel Fund issues ▼

Here are some common troubleshooting steps you can try:

General troubleshooting
👤 Are you logged in?

You need to log into the MyCebuPacific account of your booking to make changes

🔍 Can't find your booking?

You may have to add the booking to your account.

🏢 Do you have a group or travel agency booking?

Please contact your agent / coordinator directly to make changes.

✈️ Did you book on an unauthorized third-party platform?

You may have difficulty managing your booking as these platforms may issue booking reference numbers not recognized by our system.

If you encounter any issues, please chat with us to connect with a Live Agent who can verify your booking.

⏳ Is it less than 2 hours before your flight?

You cannot make any changes to flights less than 2 hours away.

Issues with CEB Flexi / Travel Fund
Did you encounter issues when paying with your Travel Fund?
  • Make sure you're logged into your MyCebuPacific account first to use your Travel Fund.
  • Please note that booking reference numbers created by travel agencies may not be used when trying to redeem the Travel Fund.
    • With this, please reach out to the travel agency where your booking was originally created.
Are you seeing this notification?

"Please enter the correct booking reference number or sign up/log in to your MyCebuPacific account to use your fund for others."
  • Double check if you are entering the correct booking reference number that contains the Travel Fund.
  • When booking a new flight, make sure the guest details match the name of the guest who owns the Travel Fund.
  • You can log in to your MyCebuPacific account so you can use your Travel Fund to book for any of your friends and family.
Are you seeing this notification?

"Sorry, that booking reference number either doesn't exist, or requires you to log in to a MyCebuPacific account."
  • Double check if you are entering the correct booking reference number that contains the Travel Fund.
  • Otherwise, please log in to your MyCebuPacific account first.
Can't see your Travel Fund? / Is the Travel Fund balance not reflecting in your MyCebuPacific account?
  • It's possible that the Travel Fund is stored in your booking reference number.
    • This happens when you choose to convert your booking to Travel Fund after experiencing a flight disruption.
  • For these cases, please do the following:
    • Log into your MyCebuPacific account.
    • Proceed to add the bookingto your account.

💡 To successfully add the Travel Fund to your MyCebuPacific account, make sure the email or any name registered in the booking matches the account's details.
