Adding and Upgrading CEB Baggage To Booking ▾

Follow these steps to add/upgrade your prepaid baggage:

  1. Log in to your MyCebuPacific account. If you don't have an account, please sign up and add your bookings to your account.
  2. Go to "Bookings and Boarding Pass" and select the booking you want to add / upgrade baggage for by clicking "Manage".
  3. In "My Booking," click "Add or upgrade bags" under "Customize Your Trip."
  4. Choose if you're adding baggage allowance per flight or for all flights.
    • If you have a round trip ticket, click "Select for both flights" toggle to ensure your baggage applies to your entire booking.
    • You may also add or upgrade the baggage allowance per person only.
  5. Select the baggage options you need. You may purchase up to three (3) pieces of 20kg baggage and add up to 12kg per piece.
    • Click "Add baggage" to add one (1) piece of 20kg baggage to your booking. You can add up to three (3) pieces of baggage per person.
    • Upgrade your weight allowance per bag, if needed.
    • If you need to bring more things, you can add +4kg, +8kg, or +12kg to each piece of baggage.
    • Just click "No" if the 20kg baggage allowance is enough.
  6. If you need to remove baggage from your selection, swipe on the baggage piece and click "Delete" or click the trash bin icon. Previously purchased baggage cannot be removed from your booking.
  7. Proceed to payment and review your transaction on the Booking Summary page. Click "Continue" to complete the payment process and confirm your booking. Note: You can also use your Travel Fund to pay for your CEB Baggage. baggage allowance per flight or for all flights.
    • If you have a round trip ticket, click "Select for both flights" toggle to ensure your baggage applies to your entire booking.
    • You may also add or upgrade the baggage allowance per person only.
Terms and Conditions ▾
  1. CEB Baggage is available for one (1) piece 20kg baggage allowance.
  2. Passengers may purchase up to three (3) pieces of 20kg baggage allowance.
  3. Passengers may also upgrade or add weight to each of the purchased 20kg baggage allowance in tiers of 4kg, 8kg or 12kg.
  4. CEB Baggage allowance may be availed through the Cebu Pacific website, mobile app, ticket offices and third-party sales offices.
  5. CEB Baggage allowance is available at the time of booking and during Manage Booking up to 2 hours before scheduled time of departure. Passengers who don't have baggage allowance can avail one (1) piece Airport Baggage up to 1 hour before departure.
  6. CEB Baggage allowance may be rebooked up to 2 hours before scheduled time of departure, subject to space availability. If a change in route is required, CEB Baggage allowance will not be carried over to the new flight.
  7. At the time of bag drop, actual baggage for check-in will be assessed against the allowance purchased (in pieces, weight and size).
  8. Maximum weight of each checked baggage piece is 32kg (as per accepted international standard).
  9. Pooling of CEB Baggage allowances is allowed provided that passengers are in the same Booking Reference and present themselves at the bag drop counters at the same time.
  10. Any excess in piece, weight and size are subject to the respective airport fees.
  11. CEB Baggage allowance may not be transferred to another person. It can only be used by the passenger for the specific flight and sector for which it is paid.
  12. CEB Baggage may not be refunded, and it cannot be stored in a Travel Fund.
