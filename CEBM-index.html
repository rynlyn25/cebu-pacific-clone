Terms & Conditions ▾
  1. Pre-ordered meals and snacks.
  2. CEB Meals may be availed through the Cebu Pacific website or sales offices.
  3. Pre-ordered meals and snacks are available for flights departing 24 hours or 12 hours from the time of booking, respectively.
  4. Sandwiches are served chilled. You may request this to be heated if the oven is available (except for Cebgo flights).
  5. A. In case of flight changes made 24 hours or more prior flight:
    Rebooking:
    • - Pre-ordered meal will be carried over to the new flight
    Rerouting:
    • - Guest Initiated: Pre-ordered meal will be forfeited
    • - Airline Initiated: Passenger has the option to refund their payments from the booking channel or store in a Travel Fund
    Cancellation:
    • - Guest Initiated: Pre-ordered meal will be forfeited
    • - Airline Initiated: Passenger has the option to refund their payment from the booking channel or store in a Travel Fund
  6. B. In case of flight changes made less than 24 hours prior flight:
    Rebooking:
    • - Pre-ordered meal will be forfeited
    Rerouting:
    • - Guest Initiated: Pre-ordered meal will be forfeited
    • - Airline Initiated: Replacement meal of the same value can still be availed on-board during the flight. Passenger also has the option to refund their payment from the booking channel or store in a Travel Fund.
    Cancellation:
    • - Guest Initiated: Pre-ordered meal will be forfeited
    • - Airline Initiated: Replacement meal of the same value can still be availed on-board during the flight. Passenger also has the option to refund their payment from the booking channel or store in a Travel Fund.
  7. Meal selections may be changed due to uncontrolled circumstances. In case pre-ordered meal is not available on the flight, guest shall be given an option to choose items of same value from the buy on board menu.
  8. Food may contain nuts, dairy and/or gluten. Refer to the menu page to see complete allergen information.
  9. Prices of pre-ordered meals may change from time to time. All prices quoted are correct at the time of booking.
  10. Change in meal selection will be allowed up to 24 hours before flight departure.
  11. Boarding pass must be presented to cabin crew during meal service.

Ceb Meals Rules

  • CEB Meals are available for pre-order online up to 24 hours before your flight. You may order up to two (2) meals per flight.
  • Light snacks and drinks are available for purchase from our Fun Cafe on board on all flights, but are subject to stock availability.
  • Sandwiches are served chilled. You may request this to be heated if the oven is available (except for Cebu Flights).
  • Allergens may vary due to the available ingredients used. The actual meal served will have the final allergen on the label.
