Seat Sale FAQS

General FAQs
1. Where can I check the destinations on sale?
You may visit our Seat Sale page to see the destinations on sale for the day.
2. What are the available payment options for this sale?
This will vary per sale. Make sure to check the FAQs for the sale that day or check our social media posts!
3. Can senior citizens and persons with disabilities avail of this promo?
Senior Citizens and Persons With Disabilities (PWDs) can avail of our domestic seat sales, but the extra citizen discount will no longer apply.
4. What does base fare mean?
Base fare means that taxes are not yet included in the fare displayed.
5. Why can't I find any seats on sale?
The destination you're looking for might not be included in the sale that day or your travel period is not covered by the promo.
