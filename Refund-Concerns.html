Refund eligibility

You may request for a refund if:

  • Your flight was cancelled by us.
  • You are a pregnant mother unfit to fly.
  • You are seriously ill and medically unfit to travel.
  • You are an immediate passenger who needs to care for an sick immediate family member.
  • You are a booked passenger with an immediate family member who has recently passed away.
  • You are an immediate family member of a booked passenger who has recently passed away.
  • Other reasons such as:
    • You were denied entry by immigration.
    • Your booking has unused government taxes.
    • Your booking has an unused Unaccompanied Minor Fee.
    • You're an exempted guest who paid for the PH Travel Tax.
    • Your card got charged twice for the same booking.
    • A double booking for the same passenger was made due to a system glitch.
  • If you have been denied entry by immigration:
    • Please approach us at the airport counters for assistance, or
    • Chat with us via our app or website to connect with a Live Agent who can help.
  • Take note! Bookings are generally non-refundable for personal reasons / reasons that don't fall under our listed criteria.
    • If you have the CEB Flexi add-on, you can cancel your booking and convert it to Travel Fund.
    • Learn more here: CEB Flexi and Travel Fund
    • For refund requests due to medical, death or bereavement causes, Cebu Pacific recognizes the following as immediate family members:
      • Parent / Grandparent
      • Children / Grandchildren
      • Spouse
      • Sibling

For unused flights, a refund is provided for the following guaranteed fees...

  • Terminal Fee
  • Taxes from the following countries: Saudi Korea, China, Malaysia, or Singapore
  • Philippine Travel Tax (PH Tax)
    • There is a processing fee when you request to refund the travel tax.

⌛ Refund processing

Refunds are processed within 18 days in the original form of payment and is subject to our fare rules.

Depending on your payment method, it may take up to 2 billing cycles for your refund be credited:

  • If it's been more than 18 days since your refund request, please fill out the Cebu Feedback Form.
    • Select "I want to know the status of my refund" and enter your booking details.
  • You may also chat with us to connect with a Live Agent who can help.

You may learn more about our refund policy by visiting our General Terms and Conditions of Carriage of Passenger, and Baggage.

FAQs

Are there any other channels where I can contact Cebu Pacific regarding my refund? ▼

For any questions or status updates regarding your refund, you can always reach out to us via our website or app. Charlie can check the status of your refund, and you may be transferred to a live agent if further assistance is needed.

You may also submit your refund status request through our Guest Feedback Form.

Do you have an email address or contact number I can reach out to regarding my refund? ▼

Our contact servers are largely virtual, so you may contact us either through the website or app. Charlie can help you in checking the status of your refund, or you may be transferred to a live agent for further assistance.

If you are an international customer from Australia, Japan, Hong Kong, China, South Korea or Taiwan.

I received an email regarding my refund. Is this legitimate? ▼

Our agents will reach out to you via email regarding your case only after:

  • You've contacted us through our official channels
  • If additional requirements are needed, and
  • You have provided your email to the agent who assisted you.

Please take note of your case number and the name of the agent who assisted you to verify that the person reaching out to you is an authorized representative.

I was contacted by an agent through social media / SMS regarding my refund. Is this legitimate? ▼

No, and we advise you not to respond. We will only contact you using official channels, and only after you have made a request through our website, app or our Guest Feedback Form. We will never use other modes of communication to get in touch with you.

Will I need to provide any personal information in order for my refund request to be processed? ▼

After you have reached out to us via official channels, we may ask you for some details, like your booking reference number (PNR), the email address associated with the booking, and other information regarding your flight and payment details.

We may also ask for identification so we can be sure it's really you making the request.

We will never ask for PINs, passwords, OTPs, or your full debit or credit card information.
