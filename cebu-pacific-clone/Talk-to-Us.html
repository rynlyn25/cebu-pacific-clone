Group Booking

If you would like to get a quote for your group:

  • Domestic: Minimum of 20 passengers
  • International: Minimum of 10 passengers

Please email us at groupbookings@cebupacificair.com with the following details:

  • Contact Person's Name
  • Company Name
  • Address
  • Contact Number(s)
  • Destination
  • Travel Date(s)
  • Preferred Flight Schedule
  • Total number of passengers (Adult/Children/Infant/PWD/Senior)
  • Other add-ons (Baggage allowance, meals, etc.)
