Bacolod ▾

CEB Transfer services in Bacolod are temporarily suspended as of February 01, 2021

Transfers Available

  • From: New Bacolod - Silay International Airport
  • To: Any CEB Transfers accredited hotels in Bacolod city (and vice versa)

Rates

Rate/s are for a one-way trip, per passenger. Infants (8 days old to 2 years old below) are free.

Notes

  • The Southwest Travel and Tour staff will be waiting for you at the exit of the Arrival hall.
  • Simply present your boarding pass or itinerary receipts to our service partners to board your CEB Transfers.

Terms & Conditions

  1. CEB Transfers is a safe and convenient transfer service to/from Bacolod airport to any accredited hotel within Bacolod city. This is in partnership with Southwest Travel and Tours.
  2. You can choose to be dropped off at any accredited hotels within Bacolod city. Please see list of accredited hotels below.
  3. Included in the transfer services are:
    • Mainland transfer (Bacolod airport to Go Hotels Bacolod)
    • Guest Coordinator Assistance
  4. Rates are quoted per guest, per one way trip. Guests need to pay twice for round trip transfers.
    Note: Infants 8 days old and below 2 years old are free of charge.
  5. Senior citizen and person with disability are eligible for discounts. Discounts are automatically computed for purchases pre-flight and inflight. Just show your ID for verification.
  6. Purchase availability depends on the direction of service
    • Airport to City/Hotels - up to 2 hours before departure in origin airport
    • City/Hotels to Airport - up to 24 hours before departure in origin airport
  7. Voucher once purchased is non-refundable.
  8. Transfer service will be spoiled for rebooking or cancellation within 24 hours before flight departure.
  9. Transfer service will be carried over to the new flight schedule for rebooking 24 hours before flights.
  10. In case of flight disruption, flights disruption policy shall apply.
  11. In case of flight delays, the provider will wait up to 1 hour after your scheduled arrival. Please contact the provider immediately if you won't make it as scheduled. Fulfillment of travel services is not guaranteed in such cases.

CEB Transfers Rules

You may purchase CEB transfers up to 24 hours before departure for flights to Boracay and Puerto Princesa through our website and sales offices.

For flights to Bacolod, Bohol, and Roxas, CEB transfers may be purchased up to four hours before departure.
