Terms and Conditions ▾
  1. Guest has the option to pre-select a specific seat based on their preference.
  2. CEB Seat Selector is available in the following categories: Premium, Standard Plus, and Standard. A specific seat category is identified at the time of booking.
  3. CEB Seat Selector may be availed through the Cebu Pacific website, mobile app, ticket offices, and as well as third party sales offices.
  4. CEB Seat Selector is available at the time of booking and during Manage Booking up to two (2) hours before scheduled time of departure.
  5. If guest chooses not to pre-select a seat, a random seat will be assigned at point of check-in. There is no guarantee that groups will be seated together or be given their seat preference.
  6. Seats designated as Exit Rows are available for pre-selection using CEB Seat Selector. The following rules apply when occupying Exit Rows:
    • Guest must be at least 15 years old.
    • Guest must be able to understand and carry out instructions which will be given in English onboard the flight.
    • Guest must be able to speak in English.
    • Guest must not be travelling with someone that requires his/ her assistance in an emergency.
    • Guest must be fit and able-bodied.
    • Guest must not have any permanent or temporary impairments (e.g. deaf, hearing impaired, blind, vision impaired, any strength or mobility limitation, intellectual impairment or travelling with a service dog).
    • Guest must be able to check outside conditions, and react to crew commands.
    • Guest must be able to reach, open, lift and throw out the exit door in an emergency.
    • Guest must acknowledge the risk runs to idling given by Cabin Crew upon onboard the aircraft. Guest must be willing to help or assists cabin attendants, in the unlikely event of an evacuation/emergency situation.
    • Guest must not be seated with an infant or a passenger below 15 years of age.
    • Guest must not require the use of an extension seat belt or have purchased an extra seat.

    Take note: Passengers seated on the exit rows must place their bags in the overhead bins. This is to ensure that all aisles are clear in the event of an emergency.

    You may keep your small valuables, such as your wallet, mobile phone, and passport with you, while your bags are placed in the overhead bin.

  7. The airline reserves the right to refuse a guest a specific seat, pre-selected or otherwise for operational and safety reasons.
  8. CEB Seat Selector may not be rebooked. It can only be used by the passenger for the specific flight and sector for which it is paid.
  9. CEB Seat Selector may not be refunded, and it cannot be stored in a Travel Fund.

Terms & Conditions for Premium Seat Category : Go Ahead Priority Boarding

  1. Go Ahead - Priority Boarding is a feature of the Premium category of Seat Selector product.
  2. Go Ahead - Priority Boarding is available to passengers on Domestic and International flights.
  3. The passenger's boarding pass, either electronic or printed must indicate Priority Boarding before this service can be fulfilled.
  4. Priority Boarding holders are required to be in the boarding gates at the prescribed time for boarding. Boarding gates open as early as 30 minutes before the scheduled time of departure.
  5. The door dedicated to Priority Boarding passengers to use the Priority Lane is at the beginning of the boarding process, as directed by the gate personnel. As soon as the lane dedicated to Priority Boarding is over, the Priority Lane may be reassigned as a regular lane for use by non priority passengers. If in use Priority Lane is not available, priority should be given first to Priority Boarding holders over regular passengers during boarding and using the regular lane.
