Pre-ordered Merchandise

  1. Pre-ordered Merchandise may be availed through the Cebu Pacific website or sales offices.
  2. Pre-ordered Merchandise are available for flights departing 12 hours from time of booking.
  3. Pre-ordered Merchandise selections may be changed due to uncontrolled circumstances. In case the item is not available on the flight, guest shall be given an option to choose items of same value from the buy on board menu.
  4. If a passenger ordered a merchandise but is not loaded, then he may choose from the BOB trolleys at an equivalent value.

Buy on Board Merchandise

  1. Buy on Board Merchandise products are available for inflight selling.
  2. Buy on Board Merchandise products may vary per flight.
  3. For inflight selling, cash and credit card transactions are accepted, but only a limited set of currencies are accepted. Accepted Currencies : AUD, CNY, HKD, JPY, KRW, SGD, THB, USD.
